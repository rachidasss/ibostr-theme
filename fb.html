You pick the number of connections at checkout, from 1 to 4, and that is how many devices can stream at the same time. You may install your playlist on as many devices as you own — the limit is on simultaneous streams, not installations. So a single login on a 1-connection plan works on your TV, phone and tablet, but only one screen plays at a time. Two people watching different channels at once needs 2 connections; a whole family usually takes 3 or 4.
